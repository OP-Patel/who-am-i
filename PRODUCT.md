# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and engineering hiring teams evaluating Om Patel for internships. They need to understand his technical range, identify relevant experience and projects quickly, and reach the underlying evidence without searching through the site.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Om's engineering experience and personal, course, and hackathon projects across FPGA systems, embedded hardware, software automation, electronics, and applied machine learning. Success means a visitor can quickly understand what Om built, what technologies he used, what outcomes were measured, and where to inspect the work in more detail.

## Positioning

The portfolio brings production software experience and hands-on digital, embedded, and hardware projects into one evidence-led engineering profile. Its value comes from specific implementations, measured results, real project media, and direct links to repositories, demonstrations, and reports rather than broad unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from an application, resume, LinkedIn profile, or shared project link. They may scan the homepage first, filter the complete project collection by technical area, open project details and image galleries, review internship experience, download the resume, and continue to GitHub, Devpost, demonstrations, or project reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript implementation deployed as a web portfolio.
- Homepage with selected projects, internship experience, capabilities, resume access, contact access, and current-work status.
- Searchable, filterable, and sortable project collection with modal detail views and media galleries.
- Project terminology and technology labels must remain technically accurate, particularly for FPGA boards, RTL, embedded interfaces, computer vision, and measured results.
- The site must remain responsive and usable with keyboard, pointer, and reduced-motion preferences.
- Future work must not fabricate project completion, performance, awards, professional outcomes, testimonials, or other evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name and personal identity remain Om Patel.
- The voice is direct, technical, concise, and recruiter-readable.
- Refinements preserve the established technical/editorial identity rather than replacing it with a generic portfolio template.
- Real project photography, screenshots, diagrams, and company marks remain the primary visual evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project records and measured outcomes are maintained in `script.js`.
- Project images, screenshots, diagrams, reports, and resume files are stored under `statics/`.
- Existing project cards link to available GitHub repositories, Devpost submissions, demonstrations, and reports.
- Internship dates, roles, technologies, and quantified outcomes are presented in `index.html`.
- The FPGA Optical DSP project is complete at functional prototype scope, with a 10-minute hardware qualification run, measured BER, timing closure, and physical capture evidence available in the project repository.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with concrete work and let the artifacts carry the story.
2. Make technical breadth easy to scan without flattening important implementation detail.
3. Pair every strong claim with visible evidence or a direct path to it.
4. Preserve accuracy over promotional language.
5. Keep navigation from first impression to project proof fast and obvious.
